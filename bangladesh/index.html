Before you go

01

Confirm the conditions

Check opening hours, access rules, transport and local notices before setting out. Historic sites can close without notice.

02

Respect the place

Follow guidance for sacred spaces, fragile ruins and private buildings. Ask before photographing people or ceremonies.

03

Leave a light trace

Carry out your waste, avoid touching masonry, and support the guides and communities who keep these places alive.
